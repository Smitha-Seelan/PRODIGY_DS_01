## Objective
To create a bar chart or histogram to visualize the distribution of a categorical or continuous variable.

## Dataset
World Bank Total Population Dataset (SP.POP.TOTL)

## Visualizations
1. Histogram showing distribution of population across countries (2022)
2. Bar chart showing top 10 most populated countries (2022)

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

I selected the total population of countries in the year 2022 as a continuous variable and visualized its distribution using a histogram. This shows how population values are spread across countries globally. Additionally, a bar chart was created for the top 10 countries to compare categorical population values.
